# Independent product source review

## Scope

Root independently reviews relationship periods, cadence, network transformations
and their accessible UI against the governing contracts. Cross-stream executors
review recovery/settings and discovery/attention. Inspect executor evidence;
do not rerun their checks merely to establish trust. Final integrated verification
is owned by integration-validation after source freeze.

## Non-goals

No production edits by the reviewer, native Obsidian acceptance claim, release,
installation, external data or real-vault changes. Preserve the existing worktree.

## Acceptance

Record actionable findings and their resolved evidence. Canonical identity,
permitted populations, explicit source-safe writes and lifecycle fences pass.
All actual features have UI, regression evidence and accurate documentation.
